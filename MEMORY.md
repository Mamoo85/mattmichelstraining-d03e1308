# DWA Strategy Memory

## Core Philosophy: Websites Are The Trojan Horse

The website is the entry point and the hook. The REAL value we deliver — and must ALWAYS stress in every pitch, email, and conversation — is the ecosystem of systems that every client gets access to.

### Every $1,499 Healthcare / Dental / Legal Website Includes FREE:
- 📱 **Review Texts** — auto-texts every happy patient/client asking for a Google review
- 💳 **Pay-Me Texts** — send a payment link via text; clients pay in hours, not weeks
- 🔔 **Appointment Reminder Texts** — automated reminders cut no-shows by 40%+

These are not upsells. They are INCLUDED at no extra charge. Lead with this. It makes the $1,499 feel like a steal.

### Add-On Systems (upsell after website is live):
- **SiteRadar** ($49/mo) — see exactly which companies and people visit their website every day
- **Trade Radar** — daily permit, storm, and homeowner signals for contractors
- **Mortgage Radar** — daily foreclosure/probate/FSBO signals for LOs and real estate agents
- **TechAlert** — hiring signals for trades companies that need to recruit
- **Advertising Management** — we run their Google Ads and Meta Ads campaigns

### In Every Cold Email and Drip Touch:
1. Lead with the personalized pain point (low reviews, no website, missing leads)
2. Show a visual — a screenshot/preview of what their site would look like
3. Include the free texting add-ons as a VALUE STACK (never bury this)
4. Mention SiteRadar: "You'll see exactly who visits your site every day"
5. End with the demo link, not just a generic landing page

### The Pitch Mental Model:
> "The website is $1,499. That's the door. But once you're in, you get our entire system — automated review collection, payment links by text, appointment reminders, and a dashboard showing every company that visits your site. Your competitors are paying $300/mo for half of this. You get it all for $99/mo."
