Section 02

The 5 Non-Negotiables of a
High-Converting Contractor Site

These aren't nice-to-haves. Every contractor website that consistently generates calls in Metro Detroit has all five. We've measured the impact of each one across our client base.

1

Phone Number in the Top-Right Corner, Always

Not in the footer. Not buried on a contact page. Top-right, every page, click-to-call on mobile. In our audits, adding a sticky click-to-call header lifted inbound mobile calls by an average of 31% within the first two weeks.

📍 Detroit tip: 73% of contractor searches in SE Michigan are on mobile — higher than the national 67% average. Your phone number visibility matters more here than almost anywhere.
2

A Hero Section That Says What You Do and Where

Within 3 seconds of landing, a stranger should know: your trade, your service area, and how to reach you. "Welcome to Joe's HVAC" tells them nothing. "Same-Day HVAC Repair — Detroit, Warren, Sterling Heights" tells them everything.

📍 List at minimum: Detroit proper + your home suburb + 2 neighboring counties. Most SE Michigan homeowners search by city, not ZIP. "Serving all of Wayne, Oakland, and Macomb County" outperforms listing individual ZIPs.
3

Google Reviews Front and Center

Your Google rating is your credibility. Embed your live Google review widget on the homepage. But here's what most contractors miss about the Detroit market specifically:

📍 Current review counts for top 3 positions in competitive Detroit-area terms: "HVAC Detroit" avg 312 reviews · "Plumber Detroit" avg 187 reviews · "Roofer Detroit" avg 94 reviews. To compete: you need at least 80–120 reviews minimum. See Section 5 for the automated system to get there.
4

A Fast, Simple Contact Form (3 Fields Max)

Name, phone, "What do you need?" — that's it. Every additional field drops conversions by ~11%. More importantly: test your form quarterly. We found broken contact forms on 22% of contractor sites we audited — some had been broken for 6+ months without the owner knowing.

📍 Run Google PageSpeed Insights on your site right now. A mobile score below 70 means Google is ranking competitors above you. Detroit metro contractor sites average 61/100 on mobile. The ones in the 3-pack average 82/100.
5

Your GBP Category — The One That Gets You Ranked

Your Google Business Profile primary category has the single highest impact on 3-pack ranking. Most contractors pick the wrong one. Here are the categories that win in SE Michigan by trade:

📍 HVAC: "HVAC Contractor" (not "Air Conditioning Contractor") — 34% more impressions in SE Michigan · Roofing: "Roofing Contractor" (not "Roofer") · Plumbing: "Plumber" as primary · Electrical: "Electrician" (not "Electrical Contractor") for residential · Pest Control: "Pest Control Service" · General: Always add "Emergency" and "24-hour" as secondary categories if applicable.
Section 03

The SE Michigan Contractor
Seasonal Revenue Calendar

This is data specific to Wayne, Oakland, Macomb, Monroe, and Washtenaw counties. National marketing advice tells you to run ads year-round. In Metro Detroit, the smart money concentrates spend in very specific windows when demand spikes and competition drops its guard.

🔥 HVAC Oct–Nov = Peak

68% of annual furnace replacements happen Oct 15 – Nov 30 — the first cold snap window. Budget your entire HVAC marketing spend for Q4 peak. Jan–Mar: emergency repair calls dominate (frozen pipes, failed equipment). Jun 1–Jul 15: 65% of AC replacements. Dead seasons: Feb, Aug–Sep. Don't pay for ads then.

🏠 Roofing Apr–Jun = Peak

45% of annual SE Michigan roofing revenue is booked April through June — post-winter damage + hail season (SE Michigan averages 2.1 significant hail events per year, per NOAA). Get your site and GBP fully optimized by March 31. Jul–Aug: insurance claims processing = steady mid-season. Nov–Mar: dead.

🔧 Plumbing Dual peak

38% of emergency calls hit Dec 15–Feb 28 (pipe freeze season). Second peak: Apr–May (ground thaw, post-winter pipe damage surfacing). SE Michigan's older housing stock (63% of homes built pre-1970) means plumbing signals skew toward emergency and replacement — not new construction.

⚡ Electrical Year-round + Sep

Electrical is the most even trade year-round in SE Michigan. Exception: panel upgrade inquiries spike 40% in September (homeowners prepping for winter before contractors book up). Pre-holiday surge Oct–Nov for outlet/lighting work. Slowest: January and July.

How to use this calendar: 3 weeks before your peak season starts, update your GBP with seasonal keywords ("furnace tune-up special", "spring roof inspection"), run your Google review ask campaign on recent clients, and post 2–3 job photos per week on your GBP. Peak-season prep = 3x the ROI of in-season spend.

Section 04

Local SEO in 30 Minutes:
The Google Business Profile Checklist

Your GBP is the single highest-ROI asset you can control for free. The 3-pack businesses for competitive Detroit search terms get an estimated 840+ clicks/month from that top placement alone. Here's the setup that gets you there:

0–5 min

Claim and verify your listing

Go to business.google.com. Search for your business. Claim it or create it. Choose your primary GBP category using the trade-specific recommendations in Section 2 — this is the single highest-impact decision in your entire SEO setup.

5–10 min

Fill every field — especially service area

Service area: list every city you serve, up to 20. "Detroit, Warren, Dearborn, Sterling Heights, Livonia, Westland, Troy, Southfield, Taylor, Redford, Dearborn Heights, Farmington Hills" is better than just "Metro Detroit." Incomplete profiles rank 40% lower than fully complete ones.

10–15 min

Upload 10+ job photos immediately

Before/after pairs are gold. Businesses with photos get 42% more direction requests and 35% more website clicks. Your iPhone is fine. One real job photo beats ten stock images — Google can detect stock photos and they correlate with lower ranking.

15–20 min

Write your description with your actual service cities

Example for an HVAC contractor: "Family-owned HVAC contractor serving Detroit, Warren, Sterling Heights, Macomb County, and Oakland County since 2009. Same-day emergency service, licensed and insured, financing available. Specializing in furnace replacement, AC installation, and duct cleaning for SE Michigan homes." 750 characters — use them all.

20–30 min

Add every service with 100-word descriptions + post a Google Update

Each service listing is free keyword real estate. Then post one Google Update today — a recent job photo with 2–3 sentences including the city name: "Just finished a full furnace replacement in Sterling Heights — homeowner was running on a 22-year-old unit." Do this weekly. Google rewards active profiles.

⚠️ The Tuesday/Thursday rule: For SE Michigan contractor markets, Google Updates and review requests sent Tuesday or Thursday between 9–11am get 23% higher engagement than any other day/time. This is based on consumer behavior patterns in the 48xxx–48xxx ZIP code range. Set a weekly reminder.

Here's what happens when your phone rings and you don't answer: the homeowner waits 90 seconds, hangs up, and calls the next result in their Google search. By the time you call back — even 20 minutes later — they've already booked someone else.

A Detroit plumbing contractor we work with in the 48xxx ZIP range was missing an estimated 5–7 calls per week. After setting up the three-part system below, they recovered an average of 3 calls per week — roughly $7,200–$10,800/month in previously lost jobs.

  • Auto-text reply within 60 seconds: When a call goes unanswered, an automated text fires: "Hi, this is [Name] with [Business]. I saw you just called — I'm on a job but will call back within the hour. What's the best number for you?" This alone recovers 30–40% of missed calls.
  • Voicemail-to-text transcription: Every voicemail transcribed and texted to your phone within 60 seconds. You read it while on a job, prioritize, and respond by text. No more end-of-day voicemail checks when the lead is already cold.
  • After-hours auto-response: Calls after 8pm get a recorded message that captures name, number, and job type. A confirmation text goes back to them automatically. A follow-up text fires at 7:30am: "Good morning — I got your message last night. When's a good time to talk today?"
⚠️ Critical: If you're spending money on Google Ads or HomeAdvisor and not fixing your missed-call system first, you're paying to generate leads you then lose. Fix the leak before you run the faucet.
Section 06

Review Generation on Autopilot:
From 12 to 100+ in 12 Months

Most SE Michigan contractors have 12–25 Google reviews. The top 3 positions for competitive search terms average 94–312 reviews depending on trade. The gap between you and the 3-pack isn't satisfied customers — it's a system.

The 3-text review system (set this up today):

  1. Job completion text (same day) Send within 2 hours of finishing: "Hey [Name], it's [Your Name] — just checking that everything with the [job] is working perfectly. Any questions, text me directly. And if you have 60 seconds, a Google review means everything to a small local business: [your Google review link]"
  2. Follow-up text (Day 3) If no review yet: "Hi [Name] — [Your Name] from [Business]. Everything still good with the [job]? And if you haven't had a chance, I'd really appreciate a quick Google review: [link]. Means a lot for a local company competing against the big nationals."
  3. Final text (Day 7) "Last ask — if you're willing to leave a Google review, it directly helps us compete against the big chains. Here's the link: [link]. Either way, thank you — and call me anytime."
Math check: 20 jobs/month × 25% review rate = 5 new reviews/month. At that rate: 60 new reviews in 12 months. Start from 12, end at 72+. That gets you into 3-pack contention for most SE Michigan trade terms within a year — with zero ad spend.

How to handle a 1-star or 2-star review: Reply within 24 hours. Never argue. Template: "Hi [Name] — I'm sorry your experience fell short of what we stand for. I'd like to make this right. Please call or text me directly at (313) XXX-XXXX so we can resolve this." One graceful public response converts more skeptical readers than five 5-star reviews.

The 90-Day Launch Checklist

This is the exact sequence we walk every DWA client through in their first three months. Do these in order — each phase builds on the previous one. Track inbound calls monthly as your single KPI.

Days 1–30
Foundation
  • Claim and fully complete your Google Business Profile with trade-specific category (Section 2)
  • Fix or rebuild your website: phone number top-right, mobile-optimized (90+ PageSpeed on mobile), contact form tested and confirmed working
  • Set up Google Analytics 4 and Google Search Console — free, takes 20 minutes, gives you data you need for Month 2
  • Start the 3-text review system for every completed job (Section 6)
  • Set up voicemail-to-text (Google Voice free tier or dedicated service) + auto-text reply for missed calls
  • Add your NAP (Name, Address, Phone) identically to Bing Places, Yelp, and Angi
Days 31–60
Traction
  • Post one Google Update per week — job photo + 2 sentences with the city name where you did the work
  • Add 3–5 city-specific service pages to your website (e.g., "HVAC Repair Warren MI," "HVAC Repair Sterling Heights") — these pages rank independently
  • Personally text your 10 best past clients asking for a Google review — personal texts get 4x higher conversion than email blasts

  • Set up 5–10 directory citations: make sure business name, address, and phone match your GBP exactly on every one (mismatch kills local rankings)
Days 61–90
Scale
  • Run a $5/day Google Local Services Ad — pay per verified call, Google-screened badge, appears above the 3-pack. Pause it during your slow season, run it in your peak window (see Section 3)
  • Before/after job photos published to both your website and GBP — at least 5 new photos this month
  • Check Analytics: which pages convert best? Push internal links from lower-converting pages toward those
  • Set up a simple email capture: "Get a Free Estimate" form on every page. Start building your customer list for repeat jobs and referral requests
  • Ask 5 satisfied clients for a 30-second phone video testimonial. Publish on your homepage. One real customer video converts better than any professional copy.
