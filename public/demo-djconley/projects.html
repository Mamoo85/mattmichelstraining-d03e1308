Featured Project

Condensing Boilers Bring About Big Savings

HEALTHCARE · TROY, MI

Southeast Michigan Medical Facility

A major Southeast Michigan healthcare facility was operating aging atmospheric boilers with thermal efficiency in the low 80s. Aging equipment meant rising maintenance costs, inconsistent steam quality, and growing concerns about reliability in a facility where boiler failure directly affects patient care.

D.J. Conley specified a new Cleaver-Brooks ClearFire condensing boiler system sized to the facility's actual load profile — not over-specified "just to be safe." The result was a dramatic jump in thermal efficiency and a measurable reduction in annual fuel spend from day one.

Food Processing · Wayne County

Food Processor Installs Economizer — $47K Annual Savings

A Metro Detroit food processing plant was venting high-temperature exhaust gases with no heat recovery. D.J. Conley performed a stack analysis and specified an inline economizer to preheat boiler feedwater using exhaust heat. The project delivered a sub-two-year payback and eliminated $47,000 in annual fuel expense.

$47K annual savings <2yr payback Economizer retrofit
