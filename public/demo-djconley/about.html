Our Story

Built on Relationships. Proven by Results.

D.J. Conley Associates was founded in 1974 by Don "D.J." Conley in partnership with Cleaver-Brooks — the dominant name in industrial boiler manufacturing. From day one, the company's model was simple: be the best local technical resource in Southeast Michigan for commercial and industrial boiler customers.

Over the decades, that model proved out. Facilities across Detroit metro came to trust D.J. Conley not just as a supplier, but as a technical partner — the team that knows their specific systems, answers the phone on a Friday night, and shows up with the right part. The business grew from new equipment sales into a full-service operation covering parts, service, rentals, energy recovery, and maintenance.

Today, under the leadership of President Pat Michels and Vice President Brian Frank — with founder Mike Conley remaining as an advisor — D.J. Conley continues to operate from its Troy, MI facility, serving the same Southeast Michigan market it has for over 50 years. The technology has changed. The commitment hasn't.

Our History

Five Decades of Southeast Michigan Boiler Service

74

1974 — Founded in Madison Heights, MI

D.J. Conley Associates established as an authorized Cleaver-Brooks manufacturer's representative serving Southeast Michigan's growing commercial and industrial market.

80s

1980s — Service Division Expansion

Added factory-trained service technicians and OEM parts distribution, becoming a true full-service boiler operation rather than just an equipment rep.

90s

1990s — Energy Recovery Focus

Expanded manufacturer lines to include energy recovery equipment — economizers, heat recovery systems, and feedwater heaters — helping Michigan facilities cut fuel costs during the energy price volatility of the decade.

00s

2000s — Troy Headquarters

Relocated to current Troy, MI facility at 2694 Elliott Drive, expanding parts inventory and service capacity to serve the growing Southeast Michigan market.

Now

Today — 50+ Years Strong

Under Pat Michels and Brian Frank, D.J. Conley continues serving hospitals, manufacturers, schools, and commercial facilities across Southeast Michigan — with the same local commitment that built the business.
