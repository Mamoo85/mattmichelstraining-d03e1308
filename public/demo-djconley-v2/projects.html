Healthcare · Troy, MI
Hospital Campus Condensing Boiler Conversion

A regional hospital campus operating aging fire-tube boilers from the 1990s faced rising natural gas costs and increasing maintenance spend. Winter peak loads were straining aging equipment with no redundancy margin.

94%
System Efficiency
28%
Fuel Savings
2.1yr
Payback Period
Solution: Specified and installed three Cleaver-Brooks condensing hot water boilers in a lead-lag-standby configuration. Condensing technology captures latent heat from flue gases — pushing system efficiency from 82% to 94%. Fuel savings of 28% exceeded projections by the first full winter season. The N+1 arrangement also eliminated the hospital's most critical single point of failure.
Food Processing · Macomb County, MI
Feedwater Economizer — $47K Annual Recovery

A large food processing facility running 24/7 steam production was exhausting recoverable heat through its stack. An energy audit showed significant BTU losses that were simply being vented to atmosphere every hour of every shift.

$47K
Annual Savings
18mo
Payback Period
340°F
Stack Temp Reduced
Solution: Installed a Cain Industries flue gas economizer on two existing Cleaver-Brooks steam boilers. The economizer preheats incoming feedwater using waste stack heat — reducing fuel demand without touching the production process. Stack temperature dropped from 340°F to 210°F. The $47,000/year in documented gas savings paid back the project in under 18 months.

Healthcare · Macomb County, MI
Hospital Steam Redundancy — N+1 Configuration

A community hospital running a single large steam boiler for sterilization, HVAC, and kitchen operations had no backup. A Joint Commission audit flagged the single-point-of-failure risk. Any unplanned outage would force immediate patient diversion.

N+1
Redundancy Level
100%
Uptime Since Install
15min
Automatic Switchover
Solution: Designed and installed a two-boiler lead-lag system with automatic switchover controls. If the lead unit fails or goes offline for maintenance, the standby unit comes online automatically within 15 minutes — with no manual intervention required. The hospital has maintained 100% steam availability since installation. The Joint Commission finding was resolved at the next audit cycle.
Commercial · Oakland County, MI
Boiler Room Modernization — 24% Operating Cost Reduction

A 400,000 SF Class A office complex was running a boiler room designed in the 1980s — oversized cast-iron boilers, manual controls, no outdoor reset, and no building automation integration. The facility manager knew they were overpaying but had no path forward.

24%
Cost Reduction
BAS
Fully Integrated
3yr
Payback Period
Solution: Replaced all three aging boilers with modulating Cleaver-Brooks units sized for actual load — not theoretical peak. Installed outdoor reset controls that modulate water temperature based on outside air temperature. Fully integrated with the building's BAS for centralized monitoring. The combination of right-sizing, modulation, and controls delivered a 24% reduction in heating operating costs — documented over the first two full heating seasons.
