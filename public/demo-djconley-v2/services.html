Preventative Maintenance Programs

Annual & Semi-Annual Contracts · Custom Schedules Available

The most expensive boiler repair is the emergency one you didn't see coming. A structured preventative maintenance program catches deterioration before it becomes failure — while simultaneously satisfying insurance requirements and extending equipment life.

We build PM programs around your specific equipment, operating schedule, and regulatory requirements. Semi-annual or annual contracts include scheduled visits, written inspection reports, and priority response if issues arise between visits.

Annual combustion analysis and efficiency tuning
Safety valve testing and certification
Water treatment review and chemical testing
Controls inspection and calibration
Written inspection reports for insurance and regulatory files
Priority scheduling for contract clients
♻️

Energy Recovery Systems

Economizers · Heat Exchangers · Condensate Return Systems

Fuel is your single largest ongoing boiler operating cost. Energy recovery systems capture waste heat from flue gas, condensate, and process exhaust — turning it back into usable energy. Most installations deliver 8–18% fuel savings with payback periods under 36 months.

We perform a no-cost energy audit to identify the highest-ROI opportunities in your system, then specify, supply, and install the right equipment. Projects range from simple flue gas economizers to full condensate recovery system redesigns.

Flue gas economizers for fire- and watertube boilers
Condensate return system design and installation
Blowdown heat recovery
Stack heat exchangers
Free energy audit and ROI projection
Michigan incentive program guidance (DTE, Consumers Energy)
