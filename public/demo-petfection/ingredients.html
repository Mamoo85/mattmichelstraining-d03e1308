We Read Every Label
So You Don't Have To.

Every ingredient in every PETfection product was chosen intentionally — for what it does, how it works, and whether Sheena would trust it on her own pet. Here's what's in our formulas and why.

What We Use

Star Ingredients & Why We Chose Them

These are the hero botanicals and natural compounds that form the backbone of PETfection's formulas.

💜
Lavender
Lavandula angustifolia
Why we use it

Lavender's calming properties are well-documented in both human and animal aromatherapy research. The linalool compounds in lavender essential oil interact with the nervous system to reduce anxiety and promote relaxation — making it a cornerstone of our calming spray and shampoo line.

Calming Antimicrobial Skin-soothing
🌼
Chamomile
Matricaria chamomilla
Why we use it

Chamomile is one of the most well-studied anti-inflammatory botanicals. Its active compound, bisabolol, reduces skin redness and irritation while supporting the skin barrier. We use it in topicals and hot spot formulas for its proven skin-calming action without harsh steroids or synthetic anti-inflammatories.

Anti-inflammatory Skin barrier support Gentle
🥕
Carrot Seed Oil
Daucus carota
Why we use it

Carrot seed oil is rich in beta-carotene (pro-vitamin A) and antioxidants that support skin cell regeneration and wound healing. We include it in our paw cream and topicals because it helps damaged skin — cracked paws, dry spots, minor abrasions — heal faster and more completely.

Vitamin A source Wound healing Cell regeneration
🌿
Peppermint
Mentha piperita
Why we use it

Peppermint's menthol provides a natural cooling sensation that soothes overheated paws and irritated skin. It also acts as a natural breath freshener through its antimicrobial properties. We use it at pet-safe dilution rates — concentrated peppermint can be overpowering for dogs' sensitive noses, so balance matters.

Cooling Breath freshening Antimicrobial
🌸
Geranium
Pelargonium graveolens
Why we use it

Geranium essential oil has documented antimicrobial properties and is effective against a range of bacteria and fungi. It's one of the reasons our bug repellent spray works — insects dislike the scent of geranium. It also helps with minor skin infections and supports skin barrier integrity in topical applications.

Antimicrobial Insect repellent Skin support
🌵
Aloe Vera
Aloe barbadensis miller
Why we use it

Aloe vera is a workhorse ingredient — deeply soothing, cooling, and hydrating. Its polysaccharides form a natural film over irritated or burned skin, locking in moisture while reducing inflammation. We use food-grade inner leaf aloe in our hot spot spray, ear cleaner, and shampoo base for its gentle, immediate relief.

Soothing Hydrating Cooling
🌾
Sesame Oil
Sesamum indicum
Why we use it

Sesame oil is one of the most deeply penetrating carrier oils available. Unlike petroleum-based moisturizers that sit on top of the skin, sesame oil absorbs quickly and carries active ingredients deeper into the dermal layers. It's also naturally rich in vitamin E and sesamolin, which provide antioxidant protection against environmental damage.

Deep moisturizing Vitamin E Antioxidant
🥥
Fractionated Coconut Oil
Cocos nucifera
Why we use it

Fractionated coconut oil (not the solid kind) is a lightweight carrier that's odorless, colorless, and stays liquid. It's an ideal base for our paw cream and topicals because it moisturizes without feeling greasy, mixes well with essential oils, and has natural antimicrobial lauric acid that supports skin health.

Lightweight carrier Antimicrobial Non-greasy
What We Reject

What's NEVER in a PETfection Product

We maintain a strict "never list." If an ingredient appears on it, it doesn't enter any of our formulas — no exceptions, no loopholes.

🚫
Parabens
Synthetic preservatives linked to hormonal disruption. We use natural preservatives instead.
🚫
Sulfates (SLS/SLES)
Harsh detergents that strip natural oils and irritate sensitive pet skin. Not necessary in a properly formulated shampoo.
🚫
Artificial Fragrances
Synthetic fragrance blends can contain hundreds of undisclosed chemicals. Dogs' noses are 10,000x more sensitive than ours — we only use essential oils.
🚫
Artificial Dyes
Color additives serve no function and some are derived from petroleum. Our products are naturally colored by their botanical ingredients.
🚫
Petroleum-Based Ingredients
Mineral oil, petrolatum, and similar compounds create a surface moisture barrier but don't nourish skin. We use plant-based oils that actually penetrate and heal.
🚫
Formaldehyde Releasers
DMDM hydantoin and similar compounds slowly release formaldehyde as a preservative. A known carcinogen — full stop.
🚫
Phthalates
Plasticizers often used to make fragrance last longer. Endocrine disruptors with no place in pet care.
🚫
PEG Compounds
Polyethylene glycols are petroleum-derived and often contaminated with 1,4-dioxane, a probable human carcinogen.
🚫
DEET or Synthetic Pesticides
Neurotoxic to dogs and cats at higher concentrations. Our bug repellent relies on geranium, citronella, and other botanical deterrents.
🚫
Propylene Glycol
A synthetic humectant that can be toxic to cats. We use natural humectants like aloe and vegetable glycerin instead.
