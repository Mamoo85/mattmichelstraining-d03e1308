Our Story

PETfection started with one product, one question, and one Michigan pet mom who refused to settle for "good enough."

How It Began

One Paw Cream Changed Everything

Sheena Michels is a pet mom from Saint Clair Shores, Michigan. Like a lot of people who love their dogs, she paid attention to what went into their food — but hadn't given the same scrutiny to what went on their skin. Until she did.

It started with Michigan winters. Sheena watched her dog's paw pads crack and dry out every cold season. She looked at every paw balm on the market and read every label. What she found bothered her: synthetic fragrances, petroleum-based thickeners, long chemical names she couldn't pronounce, and ingredients she wouldn't want near her own skin — let alone her dog's.

"I made the first Paw Cream because I wanted every ingredient to be something I could trust. Something I could read, understand, and feel good about putting on my pet. That's still the standard for every product we make."
— Sheena Michels, Founder · PETfection

So she made her own. Lavender essential oil for calming. Peppermint for a light, cooling sensation. Carrier oils — sesame, aloe, coconut — to moisturize and heal. The first batch went on her own dog's paws. They healed. They stayed soft all winter. Her friends wanted some. Then their friends wanted some.

Growing into a Full Line

That first Paw Cream became the proof of concept for everything that followed. If Sheena couldn't formulate a product to her own standard — ingredients she trusted, results that worked — it didn't make the line. Simple as that.

Today, PETfection offers a complete natural pet care system: sprays for calming and grooming, shampoos for every coat type, topicals for targeted care, eco accessories, natural treats, and curated gift baskets. Every product is still made in Michigan, still formulated with the same commitment to purity that created that first paw cream.

Available Across Michigan and Beyond

PETfection products are carried by Michigan's best independent pet retailers — from Premier Pet Supply's seven locations across the metro area to specialty shops like Grosse Pointe Pet Salon, Bubbles N Barks, Lou's Pet Shop, and Stewie's Healthy Paws. We're also available in Canada, and on Amazon and Etsy for customers who prefer to shop online.

Sheena is still hands-on in every batch and every formula decision. PETfection is still what it's always been: a small Michigan business that believes your pet deserves pure.

Our Promise

The Ingredients Commitment

Every formula Sheena creates is held to the same four standards she applied to that first paw cream.

🔍
Read Every Label
If Sheena can't understand an ingredient or wouldn't use it herself, it doesn't make it into a PETfection product.
🌿
Natural First
We always look for the natural solution before considering a synthetic one. Botanicals, essential oils, and plant extracts first.
🐾
Pet-Safe Always
Formulated specifically for dogs and cats — not adapted from human products. Pets absorb things differently and we formulate accordingly.
🏭
Made in Michigan
Small-batch production in Saint Clair Shores means freshness, quality control, and a product Sheena personally stands behind.
