## QUICK TIPS

### Don't Overthink Design
- Use your headshot for profile pics (builds trust)
- Canva for graphics (free templates)
- iPhone photos are fine for "behind the scenes" posts

### Invite Friends/Family to Follow
- Get to 20-50 followers before running ads
- Ask them to like/comment on first 3-5 posts

### Engagement
- Reply to every comment for the first 2 weeks
- DM anyone who engages and offer early access

## ALTERNATIVE: Skip Social & Just Run Google Ads

If you don't want to deal with social pages at all:

1. **Launch Google Ads today** (no social proof needed)
2. **Run ads for 30 days**
3. **Get 5-10 customers**
4. **Then create social pages** with testimonials/case studies
5. **Launch Facebook/LinkedIn ads** after you have proof

**This is actually the smartest move** — validate the product first, build social proof second.
