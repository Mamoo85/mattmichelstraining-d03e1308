Use this checklist for clients on the monthly maintenance plan.

---

- Content updates (text, photos, pricing)
- Minor design tweaks

## TIME TRACKING

Track time for each client:

**Allowed:** 2 hours (120 minutes) per month

**If client uses more than 2 hours:**
- Option 1: Bill at $50/hour for overage
- Option 2: Roll over to next month (be generous occasionally)
- Option 3: Upgrade them to higher-tier plan

**If client uses less than 2 hours:**
- Don't roll over (unless stated in contract)
- This is where you make profit

## COMMON MAINTENANCE REQUESTS

### Quick Updates (5-15 minutes):
- Update business hours
- Change phone number
- Update pricing
- Add/remove a service
- Fix a typo
- Swap out 1-2 photos

### Medium Updates (30-45 minutes):
- Add new page of content
- Reorganize services
- Update multiple images
- Add testimonials
- Create new gallery section

### Major Updates (1-2 hours):
- Redesign a page
- Add new functionality
- Integrate new tool
- Major content rewrite

**Set Expectations:**
If request will use most/all of monthly hours, warn client upfront:
"This will take about 90 minutes - that will use most of your monthly allowance. Want me to proceed?"

---

## HANDLING DIFFICULT CLIENTS

### Client requests scope creep:
"That's actually outside the maintenance plan - that would be a new feature. I can quote that separately if you'd like!"

### Client is never satisfied:
Document everything. Send before/after screenshots.
If it continues, consider ending maintenance agreement.

### Client wants hours to roll over indefinitely:
"The 2 hours are monthly - they don't accumulate. But I'm always here if you need something!"

### Client rarely uses maintenance:
Great! This is passive income. Keep them happy with monthly check-ins.

## CLIENT RETENTION

Keep maintenance clients happy:
- Respond within 24 hours
- Proactively suggest improvements
- Send occasional "just checking in" emails
- Remember their business (ask how busy season is going)
- Offer referral bonuses

**Goal:** Keep clients for 12+ months

**Revenue Example:**
- 1 client stays 12 months = $588
- 10 clients stay 12 months = $5,880
- 20 clients stay 12 months = $11,760

This is recurring revenue that builds over time!

## WHEN TO DROP A MAINTENANCE CLIENT

Red flags:
- Consistently goes over 2 hours and complains about extra charges
- Rude or disrespectful communication
- Always "emergency" requests
- Never pays on time
- Requests are always outside scope

**How to end it:**
"Hi [Name], I've realized I'm not the best fit for your ongoing needs. I'd recommend [alternative]. I'll continue service through end of this month. Thank you for the opportunity!"

**Life's too short for nightmare clients.**
