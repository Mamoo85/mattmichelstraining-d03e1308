## EMAIL BEST PRACTICES

### Deliverability Tips:
1. **Send from your personal email** (not a business domain at first)
2. **Personalize every email** - use their name, business name, city
3. **Keep it under 150 words** - busy people won't read long emails
4. **One clear CTA** - ask for a call or to view demos, not both
5. **Send during business hours** - 8am-11am or 2pm-4pm Tuesday-Thursday
6. **Avoid spam words** - free, guarantee, act now, limited time
7. **Plain text format** - no fancy HTML or images in cold emails
8. **Test with Mail-Tester.com** before sending batches

### Response Handling:
- **Interested?** → Send portfolio link, schedule call within 24-48 hours
- **Not interested** → Ask "Can I follow up in 6 months?" (keep door open)
- **Already have someone** → "Great! If you ever need a second opinion, keep me in mind"
- **Too expensive** → "I understand. What's your budget? Maybe we can work something out"
- **No reply after 3 emails** → Remove from list, try again in 6-12 months

### What to Track:
- Open rate (aim for 20-30%)
- Reply rate (aim for 5-10%)
- Meeting booking rate (aim for 2-5%)
- Conversion rate (aim for 25-40% of meetings)
