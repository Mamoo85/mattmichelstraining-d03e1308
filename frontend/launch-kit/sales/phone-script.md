# PHONE CALL SCRIPT - CONTRACTOR WEBSITE SALES

## WHEN THEY CALL YOU (Inbound from Email)

**Opening:**
"Hi [Name], this is [Your Name]! Thanks so much for getting back to me. Is now a good time to chat for about 10 minutes?"

[If yes, continue. If no, schedule a time]

**Rapport Building:**
"Great! So I reached out because I saw [Business Name] [doesn't have a website / has an older site]. I've been working with contractors here in Metro Detroit and wanted to see if I could help you get more customers online. Tell me a bit about your business - how long have you been in the [plumbing/HVAC/roofing] business?"

[Let them talk. Ask follow-up questions. Show genuine interest]

---

## DISCOVERY QUESTIONS (Ask these naturally in conversation)

### Current Situation:
- "How do most of your customers find you right now?" 
  - [Listen for: referrals, word of mouth, Yelp, Facebook]

- "Do you feel like you're getting enough calls/leads each month, or could you use more?"
  - [This reveals pain point and motivation]

- "Have you ever had a website before? What happened with it?"
  - [Learn from past experiences - why did it fail?]

### Online Presence:
- "When someone Googles '[plumber] in [City]', do you show up?"
  - [Most will say no - that's your opportunity]

- "Do you have a Google Business Profile set up?"
  - [70% of local searches use Google Maps]

- "What about Facebook or other social media?"
  - [Understand their current digital footprint]

### Business Goals:
- "If you could wave a magic wand, how many more customers would you want per month?"
  - [Get specific numbers - helps with ROI pitch]

- "What's your average job worth? Just ballpark."
  - [Calculate: "So one extra job pays for the website"]

---

## DEMO PRESENTATION

"Perfect. So here's what I do: I build modern, professional websites specifically for contractors like you. Let me show you a few examples real quick."

**Send portfolio link:** "[Your Portfolio URL] - can you open that on your phone or computer?"

[Walk through 2-3 demos]

"See how clean and professional these look? And if you open it on your phone..." [have them check mobile view]

"Everything I build is:
- Mobile-friendly - because most people search on their phones
- Fast loading - no one waits around these days
- Easy to update - you can send me changes anytime
- Focused on getting you calls - big phone number at the top, contact forms, everything"

**Ask:** "What do you think? Do you like any of these styles?"

[Let them react. Address concerns]

[Pause. Let them process]

"Which option sounds better for [Business Name]?"

## OBJECTION HANDLING

### "That's too expensive"

**Response 1:** "I totally get it. Let me ask - what were you expecting to pay?"

[If they say a number:]
"Okay, I hear you. Here's the thing - those cheap $99 websites? They look cheap. And they don't bring in customers. Think of this as an investment, not an expense. How much is one new customer worth to you?"

[Calculate ROI:]
"So if your average job is $500, you need ONE customer from the website to break even. Everything after that is pure profit. And this website will work for you 24/7."

**Response 2:** "Would it help if I offered a payment plan? $250 down, then $250 when it's done?"

---

### "I need to think about it"

**Response:** "Absolutely, I respect that. Can I ask - what specifically do you need to think about? Is it the price, the design, or something else?"

[Address their real concern]

"Fair enough. How about this - let me send you the portfolio link and some client testimonials. Take a day or two, and I'll follow up with you [Friday/Monday]. Sound good?"

[Set specific follow-up time]

---

### "I don't have time for this right now"

**Response:** "I hear you - you're busy running the business. That's exactly why this makes sense though. The website works FOR you while you're out on jobs. 

And here's the good part - you don't have to do anything except answer a few questions and send me some photos. I handle everything else.

Most of the time commitment is just one 30-minute call to get started, then you review the site before it goes live. That's it."

---

### "Can I just use Facebook?"

**Response:** "You definitely can, and it's better than nothing. But here's the problem:

1. **You don't own Facebook** - they can change the rules or shut you down anytime
2. **Not everyone uses Facebook** - especially people over 50
3. **No SEO** - When people Google 'plumber near me', your Facebook page won't show up
4. **Looks unprofessional** - Bigger companies have websites. You want to look like a serious business, right?

Think of it this way: Facebook is like renting. A website is like owning your own property."

---

### "My nephew/friend can build it cheaper"

**Response:** "That's awesome that you have someone who can help! 

Quick question though - have they built websites before? And do they know how to make it show up on Google?

I only ask because I've rebuilt a lot of 'nephew websites' where they started but never finished, or it doesn't work on phones, or no one can find it on Google.

If your nephew is a pro, great! But if this is their first time, it might be worth having someone who does this full-time and can get it done right away."

---

### "I already have a website"

**Response:** "Oh great! Can I take a look at it?"

[Look at their site]

**If it's bad:**
"Okay, I see what you're working with. Can I be honest? This site is hurting you more than helping you. It's [not mobile-friendly / super slow / looks outdated]. First impressions matter. 

What if I could rebuild this modern, professional version in 7 days? You'd start getting more calls almost immediately."

**If it's decent:**
"You know what, this actually looks pretty good. Are you happy with the number of leads it's bringing in?"

[If yes]: "That's awesome! Keep my info - if you ever want to upgrade or need help, let me know."
[If no]: "That's exactly what I fix. I can optimize it for conversions - make it easier for people to contact you."

## CLOSING TECHNIQUES

### The Assumptive Close:
"Okay great! So I'll get started this week. I just need a few things from you:
- Your logo (or I can create one if you don't have it)
- 5-10 photos of your work
- Your business info (phone, email, service areas)

I can email you a simple form to fill out. Then I'll have a draft ready for you to review by [specific date]. Sound good?"

---

### The Alternative Close:
"Perfect! So would you want to go with the Essential package at $497, or the Premium at $997 where I handle all the content writing too?"

[Either choice = they're buying]

---

### The Direct Close:
"So what do you think - ready to get started?"

[Shut up and wait for their answer]

And hey - keep my number. If you see a competitor's website you like, or if you just have questions about online marketing, feel free to call me. I'm happy to help even if we don't work together.

[Mark in CRM to follow up in 2-4 weeks]

---

## KEY REMINDERS

1. **Listen more than you talk** - Let them share their pain points
2. **Be consultative, not salesy** - You're helping them solve a problem
3. **Use their name often** - Builds rapport
4. **Speak their language** - Avoid tech jargon
5. **Create urgency** - "I have 2 spots open this month"
6. **Confidence** - You know this will help their business
7. **Handle objections calmly** - Don't get defensive
8. **Always set next steps** - Never end without a clear follow-up plan

---

## CALL TRACKING

After each call, note in your CRM:
- Date & time
- Their main concerns
- Objections raised
- Interest level (Hot/Warm/Cold)
- Next follow-up date
- Additional notes

This helps you improve your pitch and know exactly what to say on follow-ups.
