## COMMON QUESTIONS

### "What if I'm not a developer?"

You have 5 working demos. You just need to:
- Customize text
- Swap images
- Change colors
- Update contact info

**This is template customization, not coding from scratch.**

### "What if someone asks technical questions I can't answer?"

Be honest: "Great question. Let me research that and get back to you."

Then Google it or ask AI. You'll learn fast.

### "What if I mess up a client's site?"

1. You have a 7-day refund guarantee
2. You can always fix it
3. Clients are understanding if you communicate

**Everyone starts somewhere. Own mistakes and fix them.**

### "What if no one responds to my emails?"

Check:
- Is email deliverable? (Mail-tester.com)
- Are you personalizing enough?
- Are you following up?
- Are you targeting right prospects?

**Adjust and keep going. It's a numbers game.**

### "How do I know if I'm charging enough?"

$497-$997 is the sweet spot for contractors:
- Low enough they'll say yes
- High enough to be profitable
- Competitive with market rates

**After 5-10 clients, raise prices.**

## WHEN THINGS GO WRONG

### No responses after 50 emails?
- Review your email template (too salesy?)
- Check spam score
- Try different subject lines
- Target different prospects

### Can't find 50 prospects?
- You're being too picky
- Google Maps has hundreds in Detroit alone
- Lower your standards (any business without a good site)

### First client is difficult?
- Over-communicate
- Set clear expectations
- Document everything
- Learn what to avoid next time

### Can't deliver in 7 days?
- You're overthinking it
- Use your template demos
- Customize, don't rebuild
- Focus on done, not perfect
