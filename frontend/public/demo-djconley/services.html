Energy Recovery Systems

Cut Fuel Costs 15–30%

Stop Venting Money Through Your Exhaust Stack.

Most commercial boiler systems lose 10–25% of their fuel energy through the exhaust stack. Energy recovery equipment captures that heat and puts it back to work — heating makeup water, pre-heating combustion air, or supplementing building heat. The payback periods on well-designed energy recovery projects are typically 2–5 years.

D.J. Conley supplies and supports economizers, heat recovery systems, steam accumulators, and feedwater heaters from leading manufacturers. We start with a facility analysis to quantify the actual savings opportunity before recommending any equipment.

  • ✓ Economizers — recover stack heat into feedwater
  • ✓ Heat recovery ventilators for makeup air
  • ✓ Steam accumulators for swing-load facilities
  • ✓ Feedwater heaters and deaerators
  • ✓ Facility energy analysis and ROI documentation
  • ✓ Jeremias exhaust systems and stack components
