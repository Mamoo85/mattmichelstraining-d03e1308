Our Promise

We Read Every Label
So You Don't Have To

Every PETfection product starts with one question: "Would I put this on my own pet?" If the answer isn't an enthusiastic yes, it doesn't make it into the bottle.

✓ What's IN

  • Organic essential oils
  • Aloe vera & plant extracts
  • Cold-pressed carrier oils
  • Natural mineral agents
  • Herb-based antimicrobials

✕ What's NEVER

  • Parabens or sulfates
  • Artificial fragrances
  • Petroleum ingredients
  • Phthalates or PEGs
  • Formaldehyde releasers
"
I made the first Paw Cream because I wanted every ingredient to be something I could trust. Something I could read, understand, and feel good about putting on my pet. That's still the standard for every single product we make.
Sheena · Founder, PETfection Saint Clair Shores, Michigan
