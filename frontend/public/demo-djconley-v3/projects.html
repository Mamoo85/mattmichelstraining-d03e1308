Healthcare · Oakland County

400-Bed Hospital System — Dual Boiler Replacement & Economizer Package

31%
Fuel Cost Reduction

The Situation: A major Oakland County hospital system operated two aging firetube boilers that were approaching end-of-life. Both units were running at approximately 68% efficiency, with stack temperatures well above the threshold where energy recovery becomes economical. The facility was averaging three unplanned repair incidents per year — each one requiring after-hours response and carrying real risk to patient care operations.

Our Approach: We conducted a full load analysis across the facility's steam demand profile, including peak winter heating load, year-round sterilization requirements, and the hospital's planned expansion. Based on that analysis, we specified a dual Cleaver-Brooks ICS system configured for N+1 redundancy — meaning the facility would never be fully dependent on a single boiler. We added an economizer package designed to recover heat from the flue gas stream and preheat feedwater.

The Outcome: System efficiency climbed from 68% to 86% post-commissioning. Fuel costs dropped 31% in the first full year of operation. The hospital has experienced zero unplanned boiler outages since installation. Annual documented savings of $182,000 put the project on a 4.2-year payback track — ahead of the projected 5-year estimate.

Key Outcomes

31% Reduction in annual fuel spend
+18pts Efficiency gain (68% → 86%)
$182K Annual documented savings
Zero Unplanned outages in Year 1
4.2yr Simple payback period
Manufacturing · Macomb County

Tier 1 Automotive Supplier — Process Steam Upgrade & Condensate Recovery

$94K
Annual Savings

The Situation: A Tier 1 automotive stamping supplier in Macomb County was operating a single large watertube boiler for plant heating and press process steam. The system had no condensate return infrastructure — all condensate was discharged to drain. With natural gas prices rising, the plant's engineering team wanted a comprehensive review of the boiler room before committing to any capital expenditure.

Our Approach: We performed a full boiler room audit that documented fuel consumption, stack losses, condensate discharge rates, and feedwater treatment costs. The audit identified condensate recovery as the single highest-ROI project available — condensate returning to the boiler at 180°F rather than cold city water would reduce both fuel consumption and makeup water costs significantly. We designed and installed a complete condensate return system and added a blowdown heat recovery heat exchanger.

The Outcome: Condensate return rate climbed from 0% to 78%. Fuel consumption dropped 22% in the first year. Makeup water and water treatment chemical costs fell proportionally. Total annual savings of $94,000 against a project cost of $218,000 produced a 2.3-year payback — the facility's engineering team described it as the best capital project ROI they had seen in five years.

Key Outcomes

78% Condensate return rate (from 0%)
22% Reduction in fuel consumption
$94K Annual savings (fuel + water)
2.3yr Simple payback period
Food Processing · Wayne County

Regional Food Manufacturer — FDA-Compliant Steam System & PM Program

4yr
Zero Unplanned Outages

The Situation: A Wayne County food processing facility operated under strict FDA sanitation requirements for culinary steam — steam that contacts food product or food-contact surfaces must meet specific purity standards. Their existing system used a single aging boiler with no backup and had experienced two unplanned failures in the previous 18 months, each resulting in a production shutdown and product loss.

Our Approach: We specified a Cleaver-Brooks watertube system configured for culinary steam production, with appropriate water treatment and steam purity monitoring. The design incorporated a backup boiler capable of sustaining minimum production levels during any single-unit outage. Simultaneously, we implemented a quarterly PM program covering combustion tuning, safety controls testing, and heat transfer surface inspection.

The Outcome: The facility has operated for four consecutive years without an unplanned boiler outage. Annual product loss from boiler-related shutdowns, which had averaged $340,000 in the two years prior to the project, dropped to zero. The PM program has identified and resolved three developing issues before they became failures — a compelling demonstration of preventative maintenance ROI that the facility's management now uses in their capital planning model.

Key Outcomes

4yr Consecutive years, zero unplanned outages
$340K Annual product loss eliminated
3 Developing failures caught by PM before failure
100% FDA culinary steam compliance maintained
Education · Oakland County

K–12 School District — Campus Boiler Modernization Across 4 Buildings

28%
Energy Cost Reduction

The Situation: An Oakland County school district operated four school buildings with original boiler systems ranging from 22 to 38 years old. Multiple buildings were experiencing annual repair costs that were approaching 15–20% of new equipment cost. The district's facilities director needed a phased replacement strategy that could be executed within capital budget constraints over a three-year period.

Our Approach: We assessed all four buildings, prioritized replacements by condition, risk, and efficiency gap, and developed a phased capital plan the district could present to their board. The plan sequenced the highest-risk buildings first while using available utility rebate programs to reduce net project costs. All four buildings were transitioned to high-efficiency condensing boiler systems appropriate for the lower-pressure heating applications.

The Outcome: Total energy costs across all four buildings dropped 28% from the pre-project baseline. The district captured $47,000 in DTE Energy efficiency rebates that reduced total capital outlay. The phased approach allowed the work to be completed within existing capital budget allocations without a bond request — a significant political advantage for the facilities team.

Key Outcomes

28% District-wide energy cost reduction
$47K Utility rebates captured
4 Buildings modernized over 3-year phase
$0 Bond request required (fit within capital budget)
