New Boiler Sales & Specification

Cleaver-Brooks · Vapor Power

Firetube · Watertube · Electric · Rental-Ready

We begin every new equipment project with a proper load analysis. Oversized boilers cycle inefficiently and wear prematurely. Undersized systems can't meet peak demand. Getting the sizing right is the most important decision in the project, and it's where 50 years of regional experience pays dividends.

As an authorized Cleaver-Brooks and Vapor Power representative, we have access to the full product lineup, factory engineering support, and direct pricing. We specify the right system for your pressure requirements, fuel type, space constraints, and operational profile — then back it with factory-authorized installation and startup support.

  • Full load analysis and system sizing
  • Firetube and watertube configurations
  • Natural gas, #2 and #6 oil, dual-fuel
  • High-pressure steam systems
  • Low-pressure and hot water systems
  • Electric boilers for clean-room environments
  • Factory-authorized startup and commissioning
  • Complete installation coordination

Service 03

OEM Parts & Supply

Genuine Factory Parts

Cleaver-Brooks · Vapor Power · Accessories

There is no authorized substitute for OEM parts when it comes to boiler system reliability. Aftermarket components may appear compatible but often carry subtle differences in metallurgy, tolerances, or ratings that create failure risk in high-pressure, high-temperature environments. As an authorized representative, we supply genuine factory parts with the provenance to back them.

We maintain working inventory of fast-moving components and can expedite critical parts from factory stock. If you're managing an older system, we can also assist with identifying equivalent parts or evaluating upgrade paths when original components are discontinued.

  • Genuine Cleaver-Brooks OEM parts
  • Vapor Power factory components
  • Burner and combustion parts
  • Controls, sensors, and instrumentation
  • Gaskets, tubes, and pressure vessel components
  • Refractory and insulation materials
  • Feedwater and chemical treatment accessories
  • Older system parts identification support

Service 05

Preventative Maintenance

Structured PM Programs

Annual · Semi-Annual · Quarterly

Boiler failures don't happen randomly — they develop from small issues that compound over time. A structured preventative maintenance program catches those issues before they become emergencies. Our PM programs are built around manufacturer specifications and tailored to your system's age, duty cycle, and criticality.

Clients on structured PM programs consistently report fewer emergency calls, lower parts costs over time, and better efficiency than facilities that rely on reactive maintenance. The data supports what common sense suggests: planned maintenance is dramatically cheaper than emergency repair.

  • Combustion analysis and efficiency tuning
  • Burner inspection and adjustment
  • Safety control testing and certification
  • Heat transfer surface inspection and cleaning
  • Water treatment system review
  • Feedwater pump and valve inspection
  • Detailed maintenance records and reporting
  • Renewal and upgrade recommendations
Service 06

Energy Recovery Systems

Efficiency Engineering

Economizers · Heat Exchangers · Condensate Recovery

The most cost-effective energy project in most boiler rooms is not replacing the boiler — it's capturing the energy that's already being wasted. Stack temperatures above 350°F typically indicate recoverable energy. Condensate not being returned represents both a water cost and a heat cost. These recovery systems often pay back in two to four years and continue generating savings for the life of the equipment.

We design and supply economizer packages, blowdown heat recovery systems, and condensate return upgrades sized specifically for your existing boiler plant. Many projects qualify for utility rebate programs in Michigan, which we can help you document and apply for.

  • Combustion air preheaters
  • Feedwater economizers
  • Blowdown heat recovery
  • Condensate return systems
  • Waste heat boilers for process exhaust
  • Stack gas analysis and recovery potential assessment
  • Utility rebate documentation support
  • ROI modeling and payback analysis
