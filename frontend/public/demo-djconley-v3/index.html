Featured Case Study

Real Results for Michigan Industry

Healthcare · Detroit Metro

Major Detroit Hospital System Cuts Fuel Costs 31% After Boiler Replacement

A 400-bed hospital system in Oakland County had aging firetube boilers approaching end-of-life, operating at roughly 68% efficiency. Unplanned repairs were averaging three incidents per year — each one carrying serious patient care implications.

We replaced the aging plant with a dual-boiler Cleaver-Brooks ICS system, added an economizer package, and implemented a quarterly PM program. The results exceeded the projected ROI by year two.
