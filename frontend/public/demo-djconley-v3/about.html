Who We Are

A Name SE Michigan Industry Has Relied On Since 1974

When D.J. Conley Associates was founded, Michigan's manufacturing sector was at its peak and the industrial boiler market required a different kind of partner — one with genuine technical depth, not just a sales operation.

That founding philosophy — engineering first, sales second — is what earned us our Cleaver-Brooks authorization and what has kept clients coming back for decades. In an industry where relationships are built on trust and technical credibility, we've cultivated both.

Today, led by Pat Michels and Brian Frank, we continue to serve the full spectrum of Southeast Michigan industry from our Troy headquarters. The faces have changed over 50 years, but the commitment to doing the job right has not.

Company History

Five Decades of Milestones

From a small regional representative to one of Michigan's most trusted boiler systems firms — a timeline of growth built on technical credibility and client relationships.

1974 — Founded

D.J. Conley Associates Established

Founded in Troy, Michigan with a focus on commercial and industrial boiler systems for the Detroit metro area. Secured initial Cleaver-Brooks representation agreement in the first year of operation.

1980s — Expansion

Healthcare Sector Specialization

As Michigan's hospital systems expanded and modernized their facilities, D.J. Conley Associates developed deep expertise in high-pressure steam systems for healthcare applications — a niche that demanded higher technical standards than most competitors could meet.

1990s — Growth

Manufacturing & Food Processing Depth

Michigan's manufacturing sector became a core market, with the company developing process steam expertise for automotive suppliers and food processors. Vapor Power partnership established to broaden the equipment portfolio.

2000s — Service

Formalized PM and Service Programs

Structured preventative maintenance programs introduced as clients increasingly recognized the value of planned maintenance over reactive repair. The service division grew to match the scale of the equipment sales business.

2010s — Efficiency

Energy Recovery and Efficiency Focus

As energy costs rose and sustainability goals became standard for major facilities, D.J. Conley Associates built out its energy recovery capabilities — economizers, condensate return, and heat recovery systems that delivered documented ROI.

2020s — Today

50+ Years. Same Commitment.

Under the leadership of Pat Michels and Brian Frank, the company continues to serve Southeast Michigan with the same engineering-first philosophy that defined the firm from day one. Still headquartered in Troy. Still Cleaver-Brooks authorized. Still the trusted name in Michigan boiler systems.

Our Principles

What We Stand For

01

Engineering Integrity

We recommend the system that is technically correct for your application — not the one with the highest margin. Our repeat client rate over 50 years is the proof of that approach.

02

Manufacturer Authority

Authorization from Cleaver-Brooks and Vapor Power means factory-trained technicians, genuine OEM parts, and direct manufacturer support. It's a standard no generalist competitor can match.

03

Regional Commitment

We have been based in Troy, Michigan since 1974. We are not a national service company with a regional office. We are a Michigan company with deep roots in Southeast Michigan industry.

04

Lifecycle Partnership

We want to be the company you call for the specification, the installation, the annual PM, the emergency repair, and the eventual replacement. That lifecycle relationship is better for clients and better for us.

05

Honest Assessment

If your boiler needs repair rather than replacement, we'll tell you. If a lower-cost unit meets your requirements, we'll specify it. Our clients trust us because we have consistently put their interests first.

06

Responsive Service

Boiler failures don't respect business hours. Our emergency response capability exists because we know that for many of our clients, every hour of downtime carries a real cost that far exceeds the repair invoice.
