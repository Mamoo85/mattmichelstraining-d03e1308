D.J. Conley Associates has been Southeast Michigan's most trusted name in industrial boiler systems since 1974. We didn't grow by chasing every market — we grew by being the best in ours.

The Foundation

Built on Technical Integrity

D.J. Conley Associates was founded in 1974 with a single purpose: to give Southeast Michigan manufacturers, hospitals, and commercial facilities an expert partner for steam and hot water boiler systems — not a box-pusher, but a true technical resource.

That first year, we became an authorized Cleaver-Brooks representative — a designation we've held for over five decades. Cleaver-Brooks doesn't grant that status lightly. It requires deep technical training, a commitment to factory parts, and the service infrastructure to back up every system we sell.

Today, under the leadership of Pat Michels and Brian Frank, D.J. Conley Associates remains privately held, locally focused, and built on the same principles as day one: show up, know your stuff, and fix the problem.

Company History

Five Decades of Milestones

1974
Founded & Authorized
D.J. Conley Associates opens its doors in Troy, Michigan and immediately secures authorized Cleaver-Brooks representative status — the gold standard for industrial boiler expertise in the region.
1980s
Expanding the Service Footprint
As Michigan's manufacturing base grew, so did our service capabilities. We built out our parts inventory and service team to handle multi-site accounts across the Detroit metro area and beyond.
1990s
Adding Rental & Emergency Response
Introduced a dedicated boiler rental fleet for emergency and seasonal needs, allowing customers to maintain operations during planned outages or unexpected failures without skipping a beat.
2000s
Energy Recovery Systems
Expanded our portfolio to include feedwater economizers, heat recovery equipment, and efficiency retrofit programs — helping Michigan facilities reduce fuel costs as energy prices climbed.
2010s
Leadership Transition to Pat Michels & Brian Frank
Pat Michels (President) and Brian Frank (VP) take the helm, continuing the founder's commitment to technical excellence while modernizing operations, growing the parts inventory, and deepening manufacturer relationships.
2024
50 Years Strong
Celebrating 50 years as SE Michigan's premier industrial boiler company. Still family-owned, still Troy-based, still the first call for hospitals, food processors, schools, and manufacturers who can't afford downtime.
