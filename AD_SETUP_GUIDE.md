## PART 1: Google Ads Setup

### Step 1: Create Google Ads Account
1. Go to **ads.google.com**
2. Click **"Start now"**
3. Sign in with your Gmail account (`matthewmichels4@gmail.com`)
4. Skip the "campaign setup wizard" → click **"Switch to Expert Mode"** at the bottom

### Step 2: Add Payment Method
1. Click **Tools & Settings** (wrench icon, top right)
2. Click **Billing** → **Payment methods**
3. Add your credit/debit card
4. Confirm billing address

### Step 3: Set Up Conversion Tracking
1. Click **Tools & Settings** → **Measurement** → **Conversions**
2. Click **+ New Conversion Action**
3. Select **Website**
4. Click **"Add a conversion action manually"**
5. Fill out:
   - **Goal:** Purchase
   - **Conversion name:** "Industrial Database Subscription" (or "LinkedIn Ghostwriting Subscription")
   - **Value:** Use different values for each conversion
     - Industrial Database: $99
     - LinkedIn Ghostwriting: $299
   - **Count:** One (don't count multiple conversions per session)
   - **Click-through conversion window:** 30 days
6. Click **Create and Continue**
7. **IMPORTANT:** Copy the **Conversion ID** and **Conversion Label** (you'll need these later)

### Step 4: Install Google Tag (Optional but Recommended)
If you want accurate tracking:
1. Go to **Tools & Settings** → **Measurement** → **Tag Manager** (or just use Google tag directly)
2. Copy the Global Site Tag code
3. Send it to me and I'll add it to your site header

**OR** — Skip this for now and just track conversions manually based on Stripe emails

## PART 4: Monitor & Optimize

### Week 1 Checklist:
- [ ] Check campaigns daily
- [ ] Goal: CTR (Click-Through Rate) > 2% on Google, > 1% on Facebook/LinkedIn
- [ ] If CTR is low, pause and rewrite headlines
- [ ] Track signups manually (check Stripe dashboard or email notifications)

### Week 2 Checklist:
- [ ] Download search term report in Google Ads
- [ ] Add negative keywords for irrelevant searches (e.g., "free", "jobs", "software")
- [ ] Increase budget by 20% on campaigns with conversions
- [ ] Pause ad sets with 0 clicks after $50 spent

### Week 3 Checklist:
- [ ] A/B test new ad headlines
- [ ] Review cost per acquisition (CPA):
  - Industrial DB: Should be < $100
  - LinkedIn Ghostwriting: Should be < $150
- [ ] Adjust bids if CPA is too high

### Week 4 Checklist:
- [ ] Calculate ROI: (Monthly Revenue from Ads - Ad Spend) / Ad Spend
- [ ] Goal: ROI > 200% (i.e., make $3 for every $1 spent)
- [ ] Scale winning campaigns, cut losing ones
