# PROSPECT RESEARCH GUIDE
## How to Build Your List of 50 Contractors

---

## THE GOAL

Find 50 local contractors who:
1. Need a website (don't have one OR have a terrible one)
2. Are active businesses (not out of business)
3. Are in your service area (Metro Detroit)
4. Are decision-makers you can reach

---

## METHOD 1: GOOGLE MAPS SEARCH (BEST METHOD)

### Time: 2-3 hours for 50 prospects

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Go to Google Maps**

2. **Search: "[industry] in [city]"**
   - "plumber in grosse pointe mi"
   - "electrician in detroit mi"
   - "roofer in st clair shores mi"
   - "hvac in harper woods mi"
   - "landscaper in grosse pointe mi"

3. **For each result, collect:**
   - Business name
   - Phone number
   - Website URL (or "none")
   - Address/area
   - Rating (prioritize 4+ stars)

4. **Click their website (if they have one)**
   - Does it look professional? Skip them.
   - Does it look old/broken/terrible? Add them (HIGH PRIORITY)
   - Just a Facebook page? Add them (HIGH PRIORITY)
   - No website listed? Add them (HIGHEST PRIORITY)

5. **Add to your CRM/spreadsheet:**
   ```
   Business Name | Industry | Phone | Website | Status | Location | Priority
   Great Lakes Plumbing | Plumbing | (313) 555-0101 | None | NO WEBSITE | Grosse Pointe | HIGH
   ```

---

### Pro Tips:

**Target 4-5 star businesses:**
- They care about quality
- They have customers
- They have money to invest

**Avoid 1-2 star businesses:**
- They have bigger problems than a website
- Might be going out of business
- Could be difficult clients

**Skip franchises:**
- Roto-Rooter, Servpro, Mr. Rooter, etc.
- Corporate handles their websites
- Won't hire you

**Look for:**
- Family-owned businesses
- Established 5+ years
- Active (recent reviews)
- Service-area based (not retail location)

---

## METHOD 2: YELP SEARCH

### Time: 1-2 hours for 25 prospects

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Go to [Yelp.com](https://yelp.com)**

2. **Search: "[industry]" in "Grosse Pointe, MI"**

3. **Sort by:**
   - Rating (4+ stars)
   - Most reviewed

4. **Click each business:**
   - Check if they have website
   - Read reviews (are they active?)
   - Check their photos (do they post regularly?)

5. **Add to list**

**Yelp advantage:**
- See review activity (tells you if business is active)
- See photos (quality businesses post updates)
- See responses (engaged owners respond to reviews)

---

## METHOD 3: FACEBOOK LOCAL SEARCH

### Time: 1-2 hours for 20-30 prospects

**Step-by-step:**

1. **Search Facebook: "[industry] grosse pointe"**

2. **Look for:**
   - Business pages (not personal profiles)
   - Active posting
   - Good engagement (likes, comments)
   - NO website listed (or just Facebook link)

3. **Red flags for GOOD prospects:**
   - "Contact us on Facebook"
   - Facebook-only presence
   - Active page but no real website

**Why Facebook search works:**
- Businesses active on Facebook but without websites are LOW-HANGING FRUIT
- They're tech-aware enough to use social media
- But haven't invested in a proper website yet
- Easy sell: "Turn your Facebook followers into customers with a real website"

---

## METHOD 4: NEXTDOOR

### Time: 1 hour for 10-15 prospects

**If you live in the area:**

1. **Join Nextdoor for your neighborhood**

2. **Search for services:**
   - Look in "Recommendations" section
   - Search for plumber, electrician, etc.
   - See who locals recommend

3. **Check those businesses:**
   - Do they have websites?
   - Add to your list

**Advantage:**
- These are actively recommended businesses
- They have customers in your target area
- High-quality prospects

---

## METHOD 5: DRIVE AROUND (OPTIONAL)

### Time: 2-3 hours for 15-20 prospects

**If you want to get out of the house:**

1. **Drive through target neighborhoods:**
   - Grosse Pointe
   - Harper Woods
   - St Clair Shores
   - Eastpointe

2. **Look for:**
   - Service trucks with phone numbers
   - Yard signs ("Joe's Plumbing")
   - Local storefronts
   - Contractor vehicles at job sites

3. **Write down:**
   - Business name
   - Phone number
   - What you saw them doing

4. **When you get home:**
   - Google each business
   - Find their website (or lack thereof)
   - Add to list

**Advantage:**
- Very local businesses
- Often owner-operated
- May not have strong online presence yet

---

## METHOD 6: HOME ADVISOR / ANGIE'S LIST

### Time: 1 hour for 10-15 prospects

1. **Go to HomeAdvisor or Angie's List**

2. **Search your target area**

3. **Look for contractors with:**
   - Lots of reviews
   - But weak/no website
   - Active on the platform

4. **Add to list**

**Note:** Some may pay for leads on these platforms = they have marketing budget = good prospects

---

## METHOD 7: CRAIGSLIST

### Time: 30 minutes for 5-10 prospects

1. **Go to Detroit Craigslist → Services**

2. **Browse:**
   - Skilled trade services
   - Home improvement
   - Household services

3. **Look for posts with:**
   - Phone number
   - Email
   - No website mentioned

**These are often:**
- Newer businesses
- Solo operators
- People who need help with marketing

---

## QUALIFICATION CRITERIA

### GREEN FLAGS (Add to list):
- No website
- Terrible/outdated website (from 2005-2010 era)
- Facebook page only
- 4+ star rating with 10+ reviews
- Active business (recent reviews)
- Family-owned / local
- Service-based (come to your home/business)
- Established 3+ years

### YELLOW FLAGS (Maybe):
- Decent website but could be better
- Very new business (under 1 year)
- Retail location (not service-based)
- 3 star rating
- Sparse online presence

### RED FLAGS (Skip):
- Modern, professional website
- Franchise (corporate handles web)
- 1-2 star rating
- Appears out of business
- No recent activity
- National chain

---

## PRIORITY SYSTEM

Organize your prospects by priority:

### HIGH PRIORITY (Contact first):
- No website
- Terrible website
- Facebook only
- Active business with good reviews

### MEDIUM PRIORITY (Contact second):
- Outdated website
- Website exists but not mobile-friendly
- Less active online presence

### LOW PRIORITY (Contact if time):
- Decent website that could be improved
- Newer businesses
- Businesses outside core service area

## INFORMATION TO COLLECT

**Minimum required:**
- Business name
- Phone number
- Industry
- Website status

**Bonus information (if easy to find):**
- Owner/contact name
- Email address
- Years in business
- Specific pain points (old site, no mobile, etc.)
- Social media handles

**Where to find emails:**
- Their website (if they have one)
- Facebook page "About" section
- Yelp business page
- Google their business name + "contact"
- Format: info@businessname.com, firstname@businessname.com

## RED FLAGS TO AVOID

**Time wasters:**

1. **Out of business:**
   - No recent reviews
   - Google says "Permanently closed"
   - Phone disconnected

2. **Corporate/franchise:**
   - Won't hire local web developer
   - Waste of time

3. **Already has great website:**
   - Nothing for you to improve
   - Focus on easier targets

4. **Businesses with very few reviews:**
   - Might be brand new
   - Might not have budget
   - Might not have customers yet

5. **Businesses in unrelated industries:**
   - Restaurants (different sales process)
   - Retail stores (different needs)
   - Professional services (lawyers, doctors - more complex)

**Stay focused on HOME SERVICE contractors.**

## WHAT TO DO WITH YOUR LIST

Once you have 50 prospects:

1. **Sort by priority** (high first)
2. **Start with top 25**
3. **Write personalized emails** (Wednesday)
4. **Send emails** (Thursday/Friday)
5. **Research next 50** (while waiting for replies)

**Always be building your list.**

Goal: 100+ prospects in your CRM within 2 weeks

---

## COMMON MISTAKES TO AVOID

1. **Over-researching**
   - Don't spend 30 minutes per prospect
   - Get basics and move on
   - You'll learn more on the call

2. **Targeting wrong businesses**
   - Stay focused on home service contractors
   - Don't chase restaurants, lawyers, doctors (different game)

3. **Analysis paralysis**
   - "Is this the perfect prospect?"
   - Just add them to the list
   - You can qualify better after first contact

4. **Not taking action**
   - Research feels productive
   - But emailing is what gets clients
   - Set deadline: Research done by Wednesday

5. **Giving up too early**
   - "I can't find 50 prospects"
   - Yes you can - they're everywhere
   - Google Maps alone has hundreds in Metro Detroit
