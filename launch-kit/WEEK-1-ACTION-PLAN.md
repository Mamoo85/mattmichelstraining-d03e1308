## TUESDAY: BUILD PROSPECT LIST

### Full Day (9am-5pm): Research & List Building

**Goal:** Create list of 50 prospects with contact info

**Method 1: Google Maps (2-3 hours)**

1. Search "[plumber] in Grosse Pointe, MI"
2. Open each result in new tab
3. Record in CRM:
   - Business name
   - Phone number
   - Website (if any)
   - Website status (none/bad/good)
4. Do 10 searches:
   - "plumber grosse pointe"
   - "electrician grosse pointe"
   - "roofer grosse pointe"
   - "hvac grosse pointe"
   - "plumber detroit"
   - "electrician detroit"
   - "roofer st clair shores"
   - "hvac warren"
   - "landscaper grosse pointe"
   - "general contractor grosse pointe"

Target: 50 businesses

**Method 2: Yelp (1-2 hours)**

1. Search industries on Yelp for Metro Detroit
2. Sort by rating (target 4+ stars = quality businesses)
3. Add to your list
4. Note: Good reviews = they care about their business = more likely to invest in website

**Method 3: Facebook Local Search (1 hour)**

1. Search "[industry] grosse pointe" on Facebook
2. Find business pages with good engagement but no website
3. These are active businesses that need you!

**Method 4: Drive Around (2-3 hours - optional)**

1. Drive through Grosse Pointe, Harper Woods, St Clair Shores
2. Look for:
   - Service trucks with phone numbers
   - Local storefronts
   - Yard signs
3. Write down names and numbers
4. Google them when you get home to find contact info

**Priority Targets:**
- NO WEBSITE (highest priority - easy sell)
- Facebook only (second priority)
- Terrible website (third priority)
- Active on social but weak web presence (fourth priority)

**Avoid:**
- Franchises (Roto-Rooter, Servpro, etc.)
- Businesses with very professional modern sites
- Businesses outside your service area

**End of Tuesday:** You have 50 prospects in your CRM, prioritized by opportunity

## WEDNESDAY: EMAIL PREP DAY

### Morning (9am-12pm): Email Infrastructure

1. **Warm up your email account** (if brand new)
   - Send 5-10 emails to friends/family
   - Have them reply
   - This helps deliverability
   - Wait 24 hours before cold emailing

2. **Set up email templates in Gmail**
   - Create 5 drafts (one for each template)
   - Use template names as draft subjects
   - Easy to copy/paste when personalizing

3. **Test spam score**
   - Go to mail-tester.com
   - Send test email
   - Aim for 8/10 or higher
   - Adjust if needed

---

### Afternoon (1pm-5pm): Write Personalized Emails

**Goal:** Prepare 25 personalized emails ready to send Thursday

**Process per prospect (10-15 minutes each):**

1. Visit their website (or lack thereof)
2. Check their Facebook/social media
3. Find something specific about them:
   - Years in business
   - Good reviews
   - Service area
   - Something that stands out

4. Choose which email template fits:
   - Template 1: No website
   - Template 2: Bad website
   - Template 3: Local connection
   - Template 4: Educational
   - Template 5: Short & sweet

5. Personalize it:
   - Their name
   - Business name
   - City
   - Specific observation
   - Industry

6. Save as draft in Gmail
7. Mark in CRM as "Ready to Send"

**Example:**

Generic template:
> Hi [Name], I noticed [Business Name] doesn't have a website...

Personalized version:
> Hi Mike, I noticed Great Lakes Plumbing doesn't have a website, which surprised me since you've been serving Grosse Pointe for 30 years and have such great reviews on Yelp...

**This personalization is what gets responses!**

**End of Wednesday:** 25 emails written and ready to send

## FRIDAY: FOLLOW-UP & CALLS

### Morning (9am-12pm): Send Batch 2 & Monitor

1. Send next 25 emails (2 hours)
2. Check for replies from Thursday batch (1 hour)

**When someone replies:**

**Positive reply ("Tell me more"):**
- Respond within 1 hour
- Send portfolio link
- Suggest call time
- "Are you free for a quick 15-min call today or Monday?"

**Neutral reply ("What's your pricing?"):**
- Send pricing sheet
- "Happy to explain more on a quick call - when works for you?"

**Negative reply ("Not interested"):**
- "No problem! Can I follow up in 6 months?"
- Move to "Follow-Up Later" in CRM

**Question reply:**
- Answer their question
- Always end with call-to-action
- "Want to see some examples?"

## MINDSET TIPS

**You will face rejection.** That's normal.
- 90% won't respond → That's okay, focus on the 10%
- Some will say no → Move on, next prospect
- Some will be rude → Their problem, not yours

**You will feel imposter syndrome.**
- "Who am I to charge $500?" → You're solving a real problem
- "What if they ask technical questions?" → Learn as you go
- "What if I mess up?" → You have a 100% guarantee

**Stay consistent:**
- Send emails every day
- Follow up relentlessly
- Track everything in CRM
- Improve your pitch with each call

**Celebrate small wins:**
- First reply? Celebrate!
- First call? Celebrate!
- First quote sent? Celebrate!
- First client? REALLY celebrate!
