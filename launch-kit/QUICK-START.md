## 15-MINUTE SETUP

### Minute 1-5: Deploy Portfolio

**Fastest way:**
1. Go to [vercel.com](https://vercel.com)
2. Sign up with GitHub (free)
3. Click "Add New Project"
4. Upload `/workspaces/m2training/launch-kit/demos/` folder
5. Click "Deploy"

**Done.** You have a live URL.

---

### Minute 6-10: Find 10 Prospects

**Quick method:**
1. Google Maps → Search "plumber grosse pointe mi"
2. Open 10 results
3. Note: Business name, phone, website status
4. Add to Google Sheet

**Repeat later for 50 total.**

---

### Minute 11-15: Send First Email

**Use Template 5 (Short & Sweet):**

```
Subject: Website for [Business Name]?

Hi [First Name],

I build websites for contractors in Metro Detroit.

Professional, mobile-friendly, done in 7 days.

$497-$997. No monthly fees unless you want maintenance.

Here are some examples: [Your Portfolio URL]

Interested in a quick call?

[Your Name]
[Phone]
```

**Customize and send to your first prospect.**
