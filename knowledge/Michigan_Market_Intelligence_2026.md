## DIRECT MAIL: POSTCARDS vs LETTERS

### Response Rate Benchmarks (2025-2026 Data)

| Format | Response Rate | Cost/Piece | Best For |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Postcards** | 2-4.25% | $0.40-$1.00 | Quick CTA, broad prospecting, QR codes |
| **Letters** | 3-5% | $1.00-$3.00 | Complex offer, high-trust industries, personalization |
| **Oversized postcards (6x9)** | 4.25% (highest) | $0.60-$1.20 | Best of both — visual impact + detail |

### Recommendation by Audience

**For Contractors (HVAC/Plumbing/Electrical):**
- **USE: Oversized postcards (6x9 or 6x11)**
- Why: Contractors check mail between jobs. Postcards require zero effort to read. QR code → instant landing page.
- Response rate: 3-4.25%
- Cost: $0.60-$1.00 each
- Minimum viable test: 500 postcards per city = $300-$500

**For Healthcare (Nursing Homes/Staffing Agencies):**
- **USE: Letters in professional envelopes**
- Why: Healthcare decision-makers (DON, HR Director, Administrator) open professional mail. Letters convey credibility and allow for detailed value proposition.
- Response rate: 4-5%
- Cost: $1.50-$3.00 each
- Minimum viable test: 200 letters per market = $300-$600

**For Supply Houses (Demand Radar):**
- **USE: Postcards with a real signal screenshot**
- Why: Visual proof. Show them an actual signal card with a company name they recognize. "Third Coast Electric is hiring 2 electricians. Here's what they'll need."
- Response rate: 3-4%

### Key Stats
- Direct mail ROI: **112%** (highest of all media formats)
- 90% of direct mail gets opened (vs 20-30% email)
- 73% of consumers prefer direct mail for brand discovery
- Multichannel (mail + digital follow-up) lifts response by **63%**
- Personalization lifts response by **18-56%**

### The Math
- 500 postcards to HVAC contractors in Grand Rapids
- At $0.80/piece = $400
- At 3.5% response rate = 17-18 responses
- At 30% close rate on responses = 5-6 new clients
- At $125/mo avg = $625-$750/mo recurring
- **ROI: $400 spend → $7,500-$9,000/year revenue = 18-22x ROI**
