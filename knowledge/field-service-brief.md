## The Three Named Products

### 1. FieldDesk — Field Service CRM
**What it is:** Mobile-first dispatch and job management for field service crews  
**Who it's for:** HVAC, plumbing, boiler, electrical companies with 3–15 techs  
**Replacing:** eWay CRM (Outlook email tagging plugin, ~$27–40/user/mo — techs can't use it in a boiler room)  
**NOT replacing:** FieldServio (a full ERP for complex operations with rentals/parts/inventory — if they use FieldServio, they likely need it)

**Pricing:**
- $199/mo standalone (unlimited techs)
- $159/mo bundled with Detroit Web Agency website (20% off)

**Key features:**
- Dispatcher kanban board: Open → Assigned → En Route → On Site → Completed
- Live tech GPS map
- Auto-SMS to customer when tech is en route, on site, job complete
- One-tap status updates from mobile (large buttons, works in boiler rooms)
- Photo + voice note capture on-site
- Auto Google review request 2 hours after job completion

**Primary pitch angle:** "eWay is $27/user and requires Outlook. FieldDesk is $199/mo for your whole crew and works from their phone."

**Demo hook:** "What does eWay show you when your tech is en route? Nothing. FieldDesk texts the customer automatically and puts a pin on the map."

### 2. SiteRadar — Website Visitor Intelligence
**What it is:** Identifies which businesses visit a prospect's website, in real time  
**Who it's for:** Any service business that wonders where their leads come from  
**How it works:** JS snippet on their site → identifies business visitors by IP → shows company name, location, pages visited

**Pricing:**
- $49/mo standalone
- $39/mo bundled with Detroit Web Agency website (20% off)

**Primary pitch angle:** "You had 47 companies visit your site last month. Do you know who any of them were?"

**Best paired with:** FieldDesk (turn site visitors into dispatch jobs)

---

### 3. TechAlert — Hiring Monitor (The Secret Weapon)
**What it is:** Daily alerts when licensed tradespeople become available in Metro Detroit  
**Who it's for:** Field service companies that are chronically short-staffed  
**The unfair advantage:** Michigan MIOSHA publishes a public database of every licensed boiler operator, steam engineer, pressure vessel inspector, and HVAC tech in the state. We check it every morning. No other hiring tool does this.

**Pricing:**
- $99/mo standalone
- $49/mo bundled with Detroit Web Agency website (50% off — hard bundle discount, not the 20% formula)

**How it works:**
1. Scans MIOSHA license DB daily — new license = someone just got certified or changed status
2. Scans Apollo people profiles for local tradespeople showing job-seeking signals
3. Scans job boards for recent "seeking position" posts
4. AI scores each candidate 1–10 (10 = local, fresh license, actively looking)
5. Score 7+: Immediate SMS + email to matching clients
6. Score 5–6: Daily digest email

**Target roles clients can choose:**
- Boiler Operator (1st class)
- Boiler Operator (2nd class)
- Steam Engineer
- HVAC Tech
- Plumber
- Electrician
- Pipefitter
- Pressure Vessel Inspector

**Primary pitch angle:** "Michigan publishes every licensed boiler operator in the state. We check it every morning and text you when a new one shows up. The company across town doesn't know this exists."
