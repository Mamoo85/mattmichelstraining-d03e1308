## The 5 Biggest Competitive Advantages

### 1. Same-Day Fire Incident Leads (Restoration)
**Source:** Detroit Fire Department live dispatch data (2026 feed)  
**What it does:** When a residential structure fire is confirmed at an address, restoration contractors get the lead the same day — before the homeowner has called their insurance company.  
**Why nobody else has this:** DFD dispatch data is public but nobody pipes it into a lead product.  
**Typical job value:** $15,000. Lead score: 9/10.  
**Pitch line:** "The homeowner is still looking at smoke damage when your phone rings."

---

### 2. BSEED Rental License Countdown (All 11 Verticals)
**Source:** Detroit Certificate of Compliance database — 11,487 active landlord records  
**What it does:** When a landlord's rental license expires, they must pass a city inspection covering every trade — roof, HVAC, plumbing, electrical, gutters, pest, exterior, foundation, mold, debris, tree hazards. We surface expiring licenses at 90/30/7 days with the landlord's name and address.  
**Why it converts:** Deadline-driven urgency. The landlord cannot legally rent until they fix it. They will call whoever calls first.  
**Lead score:** 7 (90 days) → 8 (30 days) → 9 (7 days)  
**Pitch line:** "This landlord has 8 days to fix the roof or lose their rental license."

---

### 3. Historic District Violation Routing (Exterior)
**Source:** Detroit Historic District Commission open case database (157 active violations)  
**What it does:** When a historic district property has an open violation for roof/gutters/chimney, siding/walls, paint, or windows — we route the lead to the exact matching contractor. These must be fixed or the owner faces fines and forced remediation.  
**Why it converts:** City-ordered repairs with monetary penalties. Not "thinking about" a project — legally required to do it.  
**Lead score:** 8/10.

---

### 4. Trade Permit Neighbor Targeting (HVAC, Plumbing, Electrical)
**Source:** BSEED Trades Permit database (owner names on record)  
**What it does:** When a Mechanical, Electrical, or Plumbing permit is pulled at an address, neighbors in the same vintage housing stock likely need the same service. The permit shows the owner's name.  
**Why it converts:** Permit = confirmed job happening right now. Neighbor properties = same age = same likely failure point.  
**Lead score:** 7–8/10.

---

### 5. Same-Day SPC Storm Reports (Roofing, Exterior)
**Source:** NOAA Storm Prediction Center CSV feed (updates every 15 minutes)  
**What it does:** When storm spotters report hail or damaging wind at a GPS coordinate today, roofing and exterior contractors get the lead within hours — while homeowners are still looking at fresh damage.  
**Why nobody else has this at this speed:** SPC updates every 15 min. Angi waits for homeowners to search. We know before they've opened their laptop.  
**Lead score:** 9/10.
